# Keyboard: Use Agent7 Without a Mouse

This topic lists the keys that move you around the desktop, the windows, and the menus. You can do every task in Agent7 with the keyboard.

## 1 - Who Needs a Mouse?

The desktop has a few parts: the desktop icons, the active window, the Workspace bar at the top, the taskbar, and the notification area. Press one key to jump from one part to the next. Inside a part, the Tab key moves between the controls as usual.

## 2 - Move Between Parts of the Desktop

- **F6** – Moves to the next part of the desktop. Press Shift+F6 to move back.
- **Ctrl+Esc** – Opens or closes the Start menu.
- **Ctrl+Alt+Tab** – Switches to the next window on the desktop. Press Ctrl+Alt+Shift+Tab to go back.
- **Alt+Tab** – Does the same when your computer passes the key to Agent7. If nothing happens, use Ctrl+Alt+Tab.
- **Arrow keys** – Move between desktop icons or taskbar buttons.
- **Enter or Space** – Opens the selected icon or button.

## 3 - Windows and Menus

- **Alt** – Moves to the menu bar of the window. Press an underlined letter to open a menu.
- **Alt+F4** – Closes the window that has the focus.
- **F1** – Opens the manual of the window that has the focus.
- **Shift+F10** or the Menu key – Opens the context menu of the selected item.
- **Esc** – Closes a menu, a dialog box, or the Action Center.

## 4 - Chat and Code

- **Alt+M** – Moves to the message box of a chat.
- **Enter** – Sends the message. Press Shift+Enter to start a new line.
- **Ctrl+Shift+C** – Shows or hides the chat pane of a window.
- **F7** – Goes to the next change in Code Review. Press Shift+F7 to go to the previous change.
- **Ctrl+S** – Saves the open file in Notepad, Code, or WordPad.
