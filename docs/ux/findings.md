# Agent7 UX Findings: expert baseline

Status: baseline expert evaluation of the built app (`out/`), phase 1. One row per finding. Never delete a row; change its status.

Method: the panel driver (`scripts/ux-panel/session.mjs` and `ux.mjs`, 1280x800 screenshots), Nielsen's 10 heuristics, the Windows 7 UX guidelines (menus, dialogs, wording, keyboard access, mnemonics, focus), a keyboard-only pass (Tab, focus ring, Esc, Alt mnemonics, F1), a copy audit against `docs/DOCUMENTATION_STYLE.md`, a state audit (empty, loading, error, offline, long content) and an axe-core 4.14 scan (`scripts/ux-panel/axe-scan.mjs`, result `evidence/expert/axe.json`). The new features J25 to J31 were walked through the new start states in `scripts/ux-panel/presets/features.mjs`.

**Severity (Nielsen).** 0 not a problem, 1 cosmetic, 2 minor, 3 major, 4 catastrophe. Mapping to `rubric.md`: Nielsen 4 and 3 are rubric S1 and S2 (must fix, caps the dimension at 3 and 6), Nielsen 2 is S3 (cap 8), Nielsen 1 is S4 (cap 9 until accepted). A WCAG A or AA failure is at least Nielsen 2.

**Rubric dimension** is given in the "Guideline" column as `Dn` after the heuristic. Heuristics: H1 status visibility, H2 match with the real world, H3 control and freedom, H4 consistency and standards, H5 error prevention, H6 recognition over recall, H7 flexibility and efficiency, H8 minimalist design, H9 recover from errors, H10 help. W7 is the Windows 7 guideline, STYLE is `DOCUMENTATION_STYLE.md`, WCAG gives the success criterion.

Evidence files are in `docs/ux/evidence/expert/`. Findings of severity 2 and above have an image or the scan file. Status of every row: open (nothing is fixed yet).
